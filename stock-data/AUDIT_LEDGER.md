grep this file. If your finding appears with a status, cite it and either drop the
proposal or bring NEW data that invalidates the old measurement.

**Statuses:** `ADOPTED` (shipped, config reflects it) · `APPLIED` (correctness/UX fix
shipped, no profit bar) · `REJECTED-MEASURED` (ran on the backtest, lost — do not
re-propose without new data) · `KEPT-BY-DESIGN` (verified working as intended) ·
`USER-LOCK` (explicit user direction, not negotiable by audit) ·
`DEFERRED` (accepted open item) · `USER-SIDE` (machine/process action on the user's box).

**Standing rule:** no strategy change ships on theory — it must beat V0 on the repo's
own backtester, OOS at least baseline, risk not worse, delta > bootstrap noise.

2015-10-20 → 2026-10-16, IS ends 2024-08-07). Full detail per round:
